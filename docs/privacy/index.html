Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 26, 2026

ShortcutCycle (“we,” “us,” or “our”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and handle your information when you use our application.

Data collection

We do not collect, store, or transmit any of your personal data.

ShortcutCycle is a localized macOS utility that operates entirely on your device.

  • No tracking: We do not track your usage or usage patterns.
  • No analytics: We do not use third-party analytics services.
  • No accounts: You do not need to create an account to use the app.

Permissions

ShortcutCycle requests Accessibility permission solely to perform its core function: detecting keyboard shortcuts and switching between applications. This data is processed locally in real time and is never stored or transmitted.

Contact us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, visit our Support page to contact us.
