Frequently Asked Questions

Getting Started

How do I create a group?

Open Settings, click +, and name a group such as "Social" or "Dev." Add apps that are running now or browse for others, then press the shortcut you want, such as Option+1.

Can I customize the shortcuts?

Yes. Press the combination you want. ShortcutCycle suggests recent choices and warns if one is already in use. Add Command, Control, or Option to letter and number keys; function keys can work on their own.

What happens if an app isn't open?

The group's mode decides. Running Apps Only skips closed apps while any group app is running; if none are running, it launches the first app. All Apps launches a closed app when selected.

Automation

Can I automate ShortcutCycle with Shortcuts, Alfred, or Raycast?

Yes. Connect a group to the automation tools you already use. For example:

open "shortcutcycle://cycle?group=Browsers"

Explore all automation commands.

Can automation open Settings or my backups?

Yes. Use these commands:

open "shortcutcycle://open-settings?tab=groups"
open "shortcutcycle://open-settings?tab=general"
open "shortcutcycle://open-backup-browser"

Explore all automation commands.

Can I automate setup transfers or backup restores?

Yes. Automation can save or load a setup and bring back a backup. For example:

open "shortcutcycle://restore-backup?index=2"

ShortcutCycle checks an incoming setup before replacing anything. If it cannot be used safely, your current groups and settings stay unchanged. Explore all automation commands.

Backups & Privacy

Can I back up my setup?

Yes. ShortcutCycle automatically keeps a useful history of earlier setups. You can also export a copy whenever you want to keep one elsewhere or move it to another Mac.

Is my data private?

Yes. ShortcutCycle runs entirely on your Mac with no analytics or tracking. Your app groups and shortcuts stay on your Mac; we never see or collect them.

Compatibility & Project

What macOS versions are supported?

ShortcutCycle requires macOS 14.0 (Sonoma) or later.

Does it work with multiple monitors?

Yes, ShortcutCycle works seamlessly across multiple monitors. The HUD overlay appears on your active display, and app switching works regardless of which monitor the app is on.

Why doesn't ShortcutCycle switch to a specific window?

ShortcutCycle switches apps rather than individual windows. That keeps its permissions limited while making app-group switching fast and dependable.

Is it open source?

Yes! ShortcutCycle is 100% open source under the MIT license. You can view the code, contribute, or build it yourself on GitHub.
